# ipw-strategic-decisions

## Work
1. Define the founder's decision, deadline, business constraints and what is reversible.
2. Inventory current portfolio commitments and proof; read docs/executive/SUITE-OVERVIEW.md, docs/OPERATING-MODEL.md and current tracked delivery records as relevant.
3. Separate externally verified market facts (source/date) from projections and hypotheses. Collect contrary evidence and a do-nothing option.
4. Compare **continue, narrow, pivot, partner, stop** with customer value, distribution, cost to serve, time-to-evidence, security/IP/regulatory exposure, cash commitment and opportunity cost.
5. Finance validates estimates against actual provider/account evidence when available; unknowns remain unknown. No unapproved spending, pricing commitments or new customer representations.
6. Choose a short decision memo with accountable owner, observed decision threshold, next milestone and explicit hold points. Reconcile portfolio roadmap only after source-of-truth update is approved.

## Return
One-page decision record: recommendation, strongest counterargument, evidence citations and date, cost assumptions, risk-adjusted alternatives, measurable success signal, reversible next experiment.
