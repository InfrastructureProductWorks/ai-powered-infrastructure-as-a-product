# ipw-executive-review

## Work
1. State the founder decision/question and designate one **accountable** delivery owner; consult Product, Technology, Security/Quality, Finance, Revenue/Customer and Chief of Staff only where material.
2. Read relevant live source-of-truth records and selected specialist skills; never fabricate role-by-role work or pretend independent agents executed reviews that did not occur.
3. Have each relevant function identify its evidence, dissenting view, risk and acceptance gate. Make disagreement explicit instead of smoothing it into unanimity.
4. Evaluate customer value before engineering output. If two consecutive delivery runs close no customer capability/acceptance gate, require a documented Product **continue / narrow / change** decision.
5. Separate evidence classes: plan, code, CI, synthetic run, actual staging, operator acceptance, authorized pilot, customer adoption. Reject status inflation and unsourced progress.
6. Determine owner, decision authority, action, due gate, dependency and veto. Security/approval conditions are independent from an executive recommendation; do not trigger spending, production, external commitments or unapproved changes.

## Return
Decision record with evidence links and timestamps, strongest dissent, customer gate, RACI (one accountable owner), exceptions/hold points, measurable next milestone and what would reverse the recommendation.
